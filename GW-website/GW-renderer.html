ℹ️ About BookMaker Designer

BookMaker Designer helps you build and preview TOBj styling expressions before adding them to your manuscript.

🔧 Design

Choose from style presets or tag assemblers. Use visual controls to build TOBj expressions with colors, fonts, and margins, then copy the result.

📝 Layout

Paste your TOBj expressions and arrange content layout. Click Refresh Preview to inspect formatting.

👁️ Review

Inspect a live styled preview of your content with interactive BubbleText popups and image captions.
