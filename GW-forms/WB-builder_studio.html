ℹ️ About BookMaker

BookMaker converts your Markdown manuscript into a beautifully styled, interactive HTML eBook — no coding required.

📖 Book Config

Set your book's metadata (title, author, description), colors (page background, font, TOC, cover), pagination (auto-paginate toggle, words per page), and TOC settings (position, style, skip options). All settings are automatically saved in your browser.

📝 Template

Generate a complete .md template that includes all your configuration tags (BookTitle, AuthorName, colors, etc.) plus a chapter structure. Download it as a .md file, copy it to your clipboard, or save your settings as a .cfg file to share or reuse later.

🌐 WebBook

Upload your .md manuscript (merged with the template), preview the first 50 lines, and convert it to a fully functional HTML eBook. The output includes pagination, Table of Contents, interactive popups, responsive design, and all your custom styling.
