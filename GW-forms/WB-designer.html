ℹ️ About BookMaker Designer

BookMaker Designer helps you build and preview TOBj styling expressions before adding them to your manuscript.

🔧 Design

Choose from style presets (Full, Block, Inline, SimpleParagraph) or tag assemblers (BookSettings, ChapterSection, Image, Media, ClickWord). Use the visual controls to build TOBj expressions with colors, fonts, margins, and more. Copy the result to your clipboard.

📝 Layout

Paste your TOBj expressions and arrange your content layout. Click Refresh Preview to see how your styling looks before adding it to your manuscript.

👁️ Review

See a fully styled live preview of your content with all TOBj styling applied. Test interactive elements like BubbleText popups and image captions before copying to your manuscript.

🚀
Ready to style your book? Start in the Design tab to build your TOBj expressions. Then go to Layout to arrange and preview your content. When you're happy with the result, copy the styled content and paste it into your manuscript.
