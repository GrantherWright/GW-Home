Inline-Block Indentation Tests

__iindent - Indent entire block

This entire block is indented 2em from the left. Notice how the whole box shifts right. This is useful for nested sections or quoted blocks.


__ihanging_indent - Hanging Indent

This is a hanging indent. The first line starts at the left margin, but subsequent lines are indented 2em. This is commonly used for bibliographies or reference lists where the first line should stand out.


__ifirst_line_indent - First Line Indent

This is a first line indent. Only the first line is indented 2em, while subsequent lines start at the left margin. This is the traditional paragraph indent style used in books and articles.


__ioutdent - Outdent (Negative Indent)

This is an outdent. The entire block is shifted left by -2em, making it stick out from the left margin. This is sometimes used for pull quotes or to draw attention to a block of text.

ACT I: The Call
Chapters 1-3: The village burns. Kaelen discovers his heritage. He must leave everything behind.

ACT II: The Quest
Chapters 4-8: Journey to the Dragon's Peak. Training with the ancient dragons. Kaelen faces his first trials.

Design Notes:
The three-act structure is a classic framework for epic fantasy. Each act should escalate the stakes, deepen the character's growth, and build toward a satisfying, emotionally resonant climax.

📝 PART THREE: WRITING TOOLS
The craft of writing—style, voice, and the tools to refine them



✒️ 3.1 Style Guide

Point of View
Third person limited — Kaelen's perspective
The reader experiences the world through Kaelen's eyes, senses, and emotions. Only what he sees, feels, and knows is revealed.

Tense
Past tense — epic fantasy style
Using past tense creates a sense of timelessness and gravitas, fitting for epic fantasy. Avoid present tense unless intentionally used for immediacy.

Dialogue Style
Double quotes for spoken dialogue
Single quotes for inner thoughts
Use double quotes for dialogue to distinguish it from internal monologue. Use single quotes for thoughts to create intimacy without confusing the reader.

Voice & Tone
Epic, lyrical, and occasionally dark
The voice should feel ancient and powerful, matching the epic scope of the story. Use sensory language to create immersive, evocative scenes.

Pacing
Slow for world-building, fast for action
Use shorter sentences and paragraphs during action sequences. Use longer, more descriptive prose during world-building to immerse the reader.

Politics:
The Dragon Council once ruled Eldoria, but its power has waned. Now, the kingdom is divided between those who seek to restore the old order and those who would forge a new future.
